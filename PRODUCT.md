# Manual Vector Tracing

## Register

**brand** — marketing site and conversion landing for a focused vector-tracing service.

## Users & Purpose

Print shops, sign companies, embroidery digitizers, vehicle wrap shops, marketing agencies, and small businesses who need a blurry JPG/PNG/PDF logo turned into clean, editable, print-ready vector files (AI, EPS, SVG, PDF).

Primary job: upload a logo → get a free quote → receive a 100% manual Adobe Illustrator redraw.

Emotions: confidence, precision, production trust — “this will print clean.”

## Brand Personality

Precise · Trustworthy · Production-ready

Tagline: **Precise. Manual. Print Ready.**

## Anti-references

- Generic AI-auto-trace marketplaces and “vectorize in 1 click” tools
- Overbuilt agency dashboards, purple/indigo SaaS gradients, cream editorial magazine layouts
- Feature grids with identical icon cards stacked forever

## Design Principles

1. **Manual is the product** — every key claim should reinforce hand redraw in Illustrator, never auto-trace.
2. **Proof over fluff** — before/after, formats delivered, turnaround, and industries served beat abstract “quality” copy.
3. **One job per section** — upload / trust / why / how / proof / FAQ / contact; no hero clutter.
4. **Brand color from the logo** — committed royal blue + near-ink charcoal on clean light surfaces.
5. **Conversion-clear CTAs** — Upload / Get Free Quote as primary actions across pages.

## Voice

Direct, concrete, print-industry fluent. Short sentences. Prefer “print-ready,” “anchor points,” “manual redraw” over vague “stunning results.”
