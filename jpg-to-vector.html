JPG compression vs production needs

JPG artifacts, blur, and low resolution make logos look soft on signs, apparel, and packaging. Scaling a JPG only makes edges worse.

A manual JPG-to-vector redraw restores hard edges and true curves so the artwork stays crisp at any size.

Our manual Adobe Illustrator process

Every project is redrawn by hand — never Image Trace.

  1. Assess compression damage We note blur, blocking, and missing detail before redrawing.
  2. Interpret intended shapes Letterforms and icons are reconstructed from the best evidence in the JPG.
  3. Draw clean paths Anchor points are kept efficient for editing and cutting.
  4. Color recovery Brand colors are matched despite JPG color shifts where possible.
  5. Proof and deliver You approve a soft proof, then receive AI/EPS/SVG/PDF files.

Frequently asked questions

Straight answers about this service.

Can you vectorize a blurry JPG?

Yes — blurry and low-resolution JPGs are common. We rebuild the artwork manually and confirm unclear details with you when needed.

Do you fix JPG artifacts automatically?

We do not run filters as a substitute for redrawing. Paths are drawn cleanly so artifacts are not traced into the final file.

How is pricing determined?

Complexity of shapes and detail drives the quote. Upload your JPG for a free estimate.

Can you match Pantone colors?

If you provide Pantone or brand values, we can align the vector colors to your specification.
