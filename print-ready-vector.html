What “print-ready” really means

A file can be “vector” and still fail in production — excessive points, open paths, wrong color modes, or soft edges that look fine on screen.

We redraw with production in mind so printers, cutters, and digitizers receive artwork that behaves predictably on press and in finishing.

Our manual Adobe Illustrator process

Every project is redrawn by hand — never Image Trace.

  1. Confirm end use Print, embroidery, vinyl, screen print, or mixed use — we note requirements early.
  2. Redraw for manufacturing Paths stay clean and editable for downstream production software.
  3. Organize artwork Layers and colors are structured for practical handoff.
  4. Soft proof You approve appearance before final files are packaged.
  5. Deliver production set AI, EPS, SVG, PDF, and preview PNG for stakeholders.

Frequently asked questions

Straight answers about this service.

Which formats are included?

Standard delivery includes AI, EPS, SVG, PDF, and a high-resolution PNG preview. Tell us if your vendor needs something additional.

Can you prepare files for embroidery?

Yes. We can prepare clean vector artwork suitable for digitizing. Final stitch files are typically created by your embroidery partner.

Do you guarantee press results?

We deliver production-ready vector artwork. Final press results also depend on your printer’s process, substrates, and color management.

Can one file work for print and web?

Yes. Vectors scale for both. We can also provide PNG/SVG previews for digital use alongside print masters.
