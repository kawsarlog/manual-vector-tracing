When a logo redraw is the right fix

Many businesses only have a website PNG, a business-card scan, or a photo of printed merch. Auto-trace rarely recovers typography, spacing, or trademark detail accurately.

A manual logo redraw rebuilds shapes and lettering so you regain a usable master file — ready to brand, print, and archive.

Our manual Adobe Illustrator process

Every project is redrawn by hand — never Image Trace.

  1. Study the reference We identify letterforms, icons, and any unclear areas before redrawing.
  2. Rebuild structure Primary shapes and type are recreated with intentional paths.
  3. Refine details Corners, overlaps, and fine linework are cleaned for production.
  4. Match brand look Colors and proportions are aligned to your best available reference.
  5. Deliver editable masters Final AI/EPS/SVG/PDF files you can hand to any printer or designer.

Frequently asked questions

Straight answers about this service.

Can you recreate a logo from a photo?

Yes. Photos, screenshots, and scans are common starting points. We review the reference and tell you what can be accurately rebuilt.

Will it match my original logo?

We match shapes, spacing, and colors as closely as the reference allows. If details are unclear, we confirm with you before finishing.

Is this the same as redesigning my logo?

No. Logo redraw recreates your existing mark as a vector. It is not a brand redesign unless you specifically request creative changes.

What if fonts cannot be identified?

We can recreate lettering as outlined vector shapes that closely match the reference appearance.
