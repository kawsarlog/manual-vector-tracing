Manual Redraw vs Auto Trace

Auto-trace tools create noisy paths. Manual redraw creates production files your print partner can trust.

Auto Trace

  • Jagged edges and broken curves
  • Thousands of unnecessary anchor points
  • Hard to edit later
  • Lower print & cut quality

Manual Redraw

  • Smooth, intentional curves
  • Clean, optimized anchor points
  • Easy to edit and recolor
  • Professional print-ready quality

Frequently Asked Questions

What is manual vector tracing?

We redraw your logo or artwork by hand in Adobe Illustrator using vector paths — so it stays sharp at any size.

Do you use auto trace or software shortcuts?

No. Every project is manually redrawn for accurate shapes, typography, and print quality.

What file formats will I receive?

AI, EPS, SVG, and PDF as standard, plus PNG/JPG previews. Other formats on request.

How long does it take?

Most logos are completed in 24–48 hours. Complex or vintage work may need extra time.

Can you recreate blurry logos?

Yes. Low-resolution, pixelated, or scanned artwork is rebuilt into clean vector paths.

Do you offer unlimited revisions?

Yes, within the agreed scope — we adjust curves, spacing, and colors until you approve the soft proof.

How does pricing work?

Price depends on complexity. Upload your file for a free quote before work starts. See the pricing page for typical starting ranges.
