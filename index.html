Manual Redraw vs Auto Trace

Auto-trace tools create noisy paths. Manual redraw creates production files your print partner can trust.

Auto Trace

  • Jagged edges and broken curves
  • Thousands of unnecessary anchor points
  • Hard to edit later
  • Lower print & cut quality

Manual Redraw

  • Smooth, intentional curves
  • Clean, optimized anchor points
  • Easy to edit and recolor
  • Professional print-ready quality

What is manual vector tracing?

Manual vector tracing is the process of carefully rebuilding a raster image—such as a JPG, PNG, screenshot, scan, or low-resolution logo—into clean, editable vector artwork.

We manually recreate the shapes, curves, and typography in Adobe Illustrator so the artwork can be scaled without losing quality.

Do you use Auto Trace or Image Trace?

No. Our vector artwork is manually redrawn in Adobe Illustrator rather than relying on automatic tracing.

This allows us to create cleaner paths, smoother curves, better shapes, and production-ready artwork.

Can you vectorize a blurry or low-resolution logo?

Yes. Blurry, pixelated, scanned, or low-resolution logos are some of the most common projects we handle.

Simply upload your artwork and we'll review it before providing an exact quote. If any important details are unclear, we'll confirm them with you before completing the redraw.

What vector formats will I receive?

Standard delivery includes:

  • AI — Adobe Illustrator
  • EPS
  • SVG
  • PDF
  • High-resolution PNG preview

Additional production formats may be available upon request.

How much does manual vector tracing cost?

Pricing depends on the complexity and level of detail in your artwork.

Typical starting prices are:

  • Basic — from $20
  • Standard — from $35
  • Premium / detailed — from $60

For an exact price, simply upload your artwork and we'll review it and provide a free quote.

How long does vector tracing take?

Most standard logo redraws are completed within 24–48 hours after payment.

More complex, vintage, or highly detailed artwork may require additional time.

Rush service may be available depending on the project and current workload.

Do you offer revisions?

Yes. Revisions are included within the agreed project scope.

We can refine curves, shapes, colors, spacing, and other agreed details until the artwork meets the approved requirements.
