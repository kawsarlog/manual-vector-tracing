FAQ

Questions print shops and brands ask most

Straight answers on manual redraws, formats, timing, and how quotes work.

What is manual vector tracing?

It is the process of recreating a raster image (JPG, PNG, scan, PDF screenshot) as editable vector paths in Adobe Illustrator — by hand, not by auto-trace.

Do you use Auto Trace or Image Trace?

No. Auto-trace creates jagged edges and excess anchors. We redraw every shape and letter manually for cleaner print quality.

Can you convert a blurry or low-resolution logo?

Yes. Blurry, pixelated, and poorly scanned logos are rebuilt into sharp vector artwork. If details are unclear, we confirm critical shapes with you before final delivery.

What files will I receive?

Standard delivery includes AI, EPS, SVG, and PDF, plus PNG/JPG previews. Other formats can be added on request.

How long does delivery take?

Most standard logos are completed in 24–48 hours after payment. Complex or vintage illustrations may take longer. Rush options are available.

Do you offer unlimited revisions?

Yes, within the agreed scope — we adjust curves, spacing, and colors until you approve the soft proof.

Can you match fonts?

We match typography as closely as possible. When an exact font can’t be identified, we recreate letterforms as outlined vectors so the mark stays consistent.

How does pricing work?

Price depends on complexity. Upload your file for a free quote before work starts. See the pricing page for typical starting ranges.

Is my artwork confidential?

Yes. Files are handled securely and never shared or reused without permission.

What industries do you serve?

Print shops, sign companies, embroidery digitizers, vehicle wrap businesses, agencies, apparel brands, packaging teams, and more.
