The problem — and the outcome you need

Raster logos pixelate when enlarged. Auto-trace tools create noisy paths that printers and cutters reject. You need a file that scales cleanly and stays editable.

We rebuild your artwork by hand so you receive smooth curves, sensible anchor points, and production-ready vectors you can trust for print, embroidery, vinyl, and digital use.

Our manual Adobe Illustrator process

Every project is redrawn by hand — never Image Trace.

  1. Review your file We inspect resolution, missing detail, and intended use before quoting.
  2. Manual redraw Shapes, type, and icons are recreated with the Pen tool in Adobe Illustrator.
  3. Color & spacing check Brand colors and proportions are matched carefully to your reference.
  4. Soft proof & revisions You review a preview; we refine until the redraw is approved.
  5. Final delivery You receive AI, EPS, SVG, PDF, and a high-resolution PNG preview.

Frequently asked questions

Straight answers about this service.

What is manual vector tracing?

Manual vector tracing means rebuilding your logo or artwork by hand in Adobe Illustrator so the result is clean, editable, and scalable — not an automatic Image Trace.

Do you use auto-trace?

No. Every path is drawn manually for smoother curves and production-ready files.

What files can I send?

JPG, PNG, PDF, screenshots, scans, photos, and other common raster references are fine. Upload whatever you have for a free quote.

How long does it take?

Most standard projects are completed within 24–48 hours after payment. Complex artwork may need more time.
