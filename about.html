About the studio

Built for one job: print-ready manual vector files

Manual Vector Tracing exists so print partners and brands never have to fight auto-trace paths again. We redraw logos by hand — nothing else competes for focus.

Our story

After years of fixing broken “vectorized” files for printers, embroiderers, and wrap shops, we built a service that does only manual Adobe Illustrator redraws.

That specialty keeps quality high and turnaround clear. You send raster artwork; we return clean, editable, production-ready vectors — with revisions until the soft proof is approved.
